Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 9, 2026

195x Bench Assistant ("the App") is a guitar amplifier repair and diagnostics tool developed by Brent Wrisley ("we", "us", "our"). This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

Account information: When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo. We use this to create and identify your account.

User content: The App stores data you create, including bench jobs, amplifier profiles, measurements, repair actions, tech notes, customer information, chat messages, and schematic references. This data is associated with your account.

Usage data: We track token usage (the volume of AI chat interactions) for quota management. We do not track screen views, tap events, or behavioral analytics.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the core App functionality (job tracking, AI troubleshooting, reference tools)
  • To send your chat messages to OpenAI's API for AI-powered responses
  • To manage your account and enforce usage quotas
  • To improve the App (aggregate, non-identifying usage patterns only)

Third-Party Services

OpenAI: Chat messages you send are processed by OpenAI's API to generate AI responses. OpenAI's data usage policies apply to this processing. We do not send your personal information (name, email) to OpenAI — only your chat message content and relevant job context.

Google / Apple: Authentication is handled by Google OAuth and/or Sign in with Apple. We receive only the profile information listed above.

Sentry: We use Sentry for error tracking. When the App encounters an error, a crash report is sent that may include device type and error context. No personal information or chat content is included in crash reports.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Neon (via Replit). Custom API keys, if provided, are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM. All data is transmitted over HTTPS.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You can delete your entire account and all associated data at any time from the Profile screen in the App. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your Rights

You can access, modify, or delete your data through the App at any time. To request a complete data export or to ask questions about your data, contact us at the email below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.
