Getting Help

If you're experiencing issues with the 195x Bench Assistant app, here's how to get help:

Common Questions

How do I delete my account?
Open the app → tap your name at the top of the Home screen → Profile → scroll to "Account" → Delete Account.

The AI assistant won't respond.
Check your internet connection. If the issue persists, your monthly token quota may have been reached. You can add your own OpenAI API key in Profile → OpenAI API Key.

I can't log in.
The app requires a Google or Apple account. If you're stuck on the "Pending Approval" screen, your account is waiting for admin approval. Contact us below.

How do I use voice input?
In any chat, tap the microphone icon next to the text input. Grant microphone and speech recognition permissions when prompted. Speak your question — the transcription appears in real time.
